Opportunities.

Grants, open calls, residencies, commissions and prizes for creative people, from the EU, the UK, Italy, Slovenia and beyond. Checked every morning and matched to what you do.

How it works

  1. Say what you do

    Answer two quick questions: what kind of work you do and where you're based. That's all it needs.

  2. See what fits

    Every morning dozens of funders, foundations and open-call sites are checked. You see the calls that fit you, with the deadline, the money and why each one is there.

  3. Save the good ones

    Press Save to keep a call in your Saved list, sorted by deadline. Not for me hides it. Everything stays private in your browser.

Two quick questions

You can change your answers any time.

1. What do you do? Tap all that apply
